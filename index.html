  • How your fare is worked out: whole car journey = ₦150 + km x ₦300. Your fare per seat = whole car journey divided by the riders sharing, rounded to the nearest naira.
  • You can paste coordinates (like 6.4281, 3.4219), a Google Maps link that shows coordinates, or a plus code. Short links such as maps.app.goo.gl cannot be opened from this page.
